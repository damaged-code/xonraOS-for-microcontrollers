xonraOS App Store

Small apps for a tiny ESP8266 + OLED computer. Try each one right here, then install it on your board from its Store app.

Install on your board

  1. Set STORE_URL in secrets.h to this site's address and flash the firmware (see the project page).
  2. On the board open Store, move to an app, and hold the main button. GET installs, UPD updates, OK removes.
  3. The app appears on the Home screen.

The preview approximates the 128×64 screen in your browser: fonts and timing differ a little from the real board. Buttons: Tap and Hold are the main button, D6 is the second button, and the slider is the knob.

Open source: github.com/damaged-code/xonraOS-for-microcontrollers. Want your app here? Open a pull request that adds server/apps/<id>.js and a line in server/manifest.txt. Apps are reviewed before they are merged, but only install apps you trust.
